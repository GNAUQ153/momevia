This version contains 50 externally authored music files: 44 CC0-1.0 compositions from Alex McCulloch / Pro Sensory and Emma_MA, and six Korean recordings supplied by the Korea Copyright Commission under attribution terms.
The six Korean files are cross-checked against their rightsholder pages and Wikimedia Commons CC BY 4.0 declarations. The 2021 Arirang vocal recording uses the KCC unrestricted-donation terms with work, author, source and terms attribution; that distinction is recorded in its evidence JSON.
Full artist credits, license links, sources and processing changes must accompany playback. The product displays these in Studio and the recipient player.
See catalog.json and ../../audio-licenses/CREDITS.md for original titles, author URLs, source hashes and processing notes.
Momevia did not synthesize these compositions.
